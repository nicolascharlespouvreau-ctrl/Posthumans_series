What would you pay to never die? And who would pay it for you?

Two humanities split apart. The chosen live above a toxic cloud and no longer die. The naturals stay below and endure. One man walks between them, chasing a name he doesn't understand.

For fans of Blade Runner, Altered Carbon and Total Recall.

The goal: a real series. Real actors, a real crew, real sets. The images and the teaser you see here are AI-made concept art, built to show the world before asking for anything. If the vote succeeds, the series will be shot for real.

Field notes

The world so far

Location file
The Esplanade

Nobody built this place to be beautiful. It just ended up that way, the way rot sometimes does. The lights never go out here. Nobody knows who's still paying for them.

"Something still watches from the top."

Location file
Totengruft

Everything else down here runs on scraps and hope. This place never flickers, never dims, never runs out. Someone's paying for that. Nobody down here can afford to ask who.

"Some lights never go out. Ask yourself why."

Fragment
The Watcher

Nobody built it. Nobody claims it. It's just always been there, watching the canyon with empty eyes, before the fall or after. Nobody agrees which.

"Nobody remembers who carved it. Or why."

The author

Meet the creator

I spent twenty years in uniform. Now I'm building a world.

I spent twenty years in the French Army, in the Troupes de Marine, with overseas deployments including Afghanistan and Lebanon. Then I changed course and moved into cinema, with roles in films and series.

Posthumans is the story I finally decided to write. The goal is simple: make it for real, with real actors and a real crew.
